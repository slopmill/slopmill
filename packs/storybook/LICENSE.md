Milne died in 1956. His books entered the public domain in the United States 95 years
after publication; in the United Kingdom and many other countries they stay in copyright
until the end of 2026. Outside the United States, check before you copy the samples.

This pack shows what a voice pack is. It is not an invitation to imitate famous writers:
the point of slopmill is to write in your own voice. The samples and the voice file quote
Milne's own text, characters' names included, from books that are public domain in the
United States. Disney's later versions of those characters (its pictures, designs and
trademarks) are Disney's; this pack uses none of them.
