# Voice packs and their licenses

A voice pack is writing: a brief, some rules, and samples of somebody's prose. It is not
code, so the code licenses in this repository do not cover it. Each voice pack says what
you may do with it in its own `LICENSE.md`.

**Voice packs you make are yours.** slopmill claims nothing in your samples, your rules or
anything the model writes for you. Your packs are stored in your `workspace/voices/` folder.
Every writing pass sends the pack, with the issue, to the AI service you chose: that is how
the model learns your voice, so only upload writing you are happy to send there.

**When you share one**, put a `LICENSE.md` in its folder that says, for each file:

1. who wrote it,
2. the license you are giving (for example CC BY 4.0, CC0, or "all rights reserved: for
   reading only"),
3. for samples that are someone else's writing, why you may share them (your own work,
   their permission, or the public domain, and where).

**The example pack in this repository** (`packs/storybook/voice/`) is licensed in its
own `packs/storybook/LICENSE.md`: the writer file and the voice file are CC BY 4.0
(`LICENSES/CC-BY-4.0.txt`); the samples are A. A. Milne's, published 1919 to 1928, and are
in the public domain in the United States.

**What voice packs are for.** A voice pack is how you get a model to write the way *you*
write. Please do not use slopmill to pass off imitations of other living writers as their
work, or yours.
